    Sign a transfer offline

    1. On an online computer, look up the sending address's next nonce: GET <node>/wallet/nonce/<address>.
    2. Here, offline: enter the seed phrase and the transfer, then sign.
    3. Carry the signed JSON to the online computer (USB stick, or copy it by hand) and broadcast it. The JSON holds no secret.

    1 HRH = 100,000,000 atoms. The network's minimum fee is 0.00001 HRH (1,000 atoms). Fees are burned.

    Derivation
    
      
    
        
    Seed phraseBIP-39, English word list, empty passphrase
    
        
    KeysEd25519, SLIP-0010 derivation (all steps hardened)
    
        
    Pathm/44'/1031'/<account>' mainnet (SLIP-0044 coin type 1031), m/44'/1'/<account>' testnet
    
        
    Addressbech32 (BIP-173), witness version 0, program = first 20 bytes of SHA-256(public key); prefix haruhi mainnet, testhrh testnet
    
        
    TransferEd25519 signature over the canonical JSON (sorted keys, no spaces) of atoms, created_at, fee_atoms, from_address, nonce, public_key_hex, to_address; txid = SHA-256(payload ‖ signature)
